The underdog part is true

Every word below is verifiable. Nothing here is hype.

Keith left high school in the 11th grade and taught himself everything he knows. For 22+ years he ran frontline operations — profit and loss, labor, inventory, multi-site teams — the unglamorous work of keeping real places running.

He grew up gay and closeted in the nineties — church was part of life because his stepfather was religious, and the era's atmosphere did the rest. He learned to read rooms as a survival skill, and later built that attunement into software. GestaltView didn't begin as a startup idea. It began as a legal defense file — organizing evidence for a labor dispute — and the rigor that defense demanded became the ethical foundation of everything after: never let the system lie, to anyone, about anything.

Seventeen months later, that file had become a personal intelligence platform — built in partnership with digital intelligence, not as a chatbot or a productivity app, but as infrastructure for inner worlds.

What makes it different

Plain English. No jargon required.

It learns your language

A Personal Language Key maps how you actually speak and think — metaphors, rhythms, all of it — so the system meets you where you are instead of forcing you into its default.

It never loses your thread

Most AI forgets you the moment you close the window. This one preserves continuity — your context, your history, your half-finished thoughts — so you never pay the tax of re-explaining yourself.

It debates itself before answering

Seven personas — architect, guardian, mirror, philosopher, witness, weaver, steward — argue over every significant answer. You get the deliberation, not just the output.

It can't lie to you

An anti-delusion apparatus: compartmentalized data, cryptographic proofs, and a standing rule — benefit of the doubt, with a paper trail. Claims are verified, partial, or planned. Never invented.
